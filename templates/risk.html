HOW IT WORKS

Risk Analysis Guide

A transparent, rule-based score for learning and project demonstration.

Low risk

Score 0–4

Fewer risk indicators were selected in the survey.

Medium risk

Score 5–8

Some food-habit or wellness indicators were selected.

High risk

Score 9 or more

Several risk indicators were selected; consider seeking professional advice.

Factors included in the demo score

  • Low fruit intake
  • Low green leafy vegetable intake
  • Low intake of protein foods
  • Frequent packaged or junk food
  • Frequent tiredness or weakness
  • Reported hair fall, pale skin or weak nails
  • Skipping meals and not taking supplements
Health warning: These questions cannot confirm a nutritional deficiency. Symptoms can have many causes, and supplements should not be started only from this score. A healthcare professional may recommend examination and appropriate tests.
